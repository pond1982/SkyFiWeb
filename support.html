Support

SkyFi runs entirely on-device — most issues are resolved on your phone in under a minute.

Need help fast?

Email support@skyfi.example with your device model, iOS version, and a short description (or screenshot) of what you're seeing. We aim to respond within 2 business days.

Getting started

How do I use SkyFi?

Open the app and grant camera access when prompted. The live preview appears immediately. The bottom of the screen has:

  • Lens carousel — tap to switch between Original Feed, Chaotic Sky, UFO Monolith, and Debug Sky Tint.
  • Shutter button (center) — tap once to start recording, tap again to stop and save to Photos.
  • Aspect button (left) — toggle between 9:16 portrait and 16:9 horizontal recording.
  • Camera flip button (right) — switch between the back and front camera.

The top-left has a FLIR / COLOR chip that flips the entire frame between full color and Navy-FLIR-style black-and-white.

Which lens should I try first?

Chaotic Sky + FLIR is the most cinematic — point your phone at an open sky to see three glowing UAPs drift over a B&W cityscape with FLIR targeting brackets. Best at dusk or against an open horizon.

Common issues

Why does it say "NO SKY DETECTED"?

SkyFi's neural network couldn't find any sky pixels in the current frame. This is the correct (honest) behavior when:

  • You're pointing the camera at a wall, ceiling, or floor.
  • You're indoors with no visible window.
  • It's very dark or the scene is heavily backlit.
  • The frame is mostly buildings or foliage with no open sky.

Point the camera somewhere that includes actual sky — even a small patch through trees — and the indicator will switch back to LIVE automatically.

The preview is laggy / dropping frames.

SkyFi displays at 60 fps independently of the AI inference rate, so the live preview should always be smooth even if the sky mask updates a few times per second. If you see actual frame drops:

  • Check the top-right HUD for a THERMAL: HIGH or THERMAL: CRITICAL indicator. Under heavy thermal load SkyFi automatically reduces segmentation rate to protect your device — give it 30 seconds to cool down.
  • Close other camera apps in the background.
  • Older devices (pre-iPhone XS) lack the Apple Neural Engine and rely on the GPU, which is slower for this model.
The lens effect "lags" behind when I move the phone.

This is expected. The neural network re-segments the sky every ~100 ms, but the live camera preview updates every ~16 ms. For static or slowly-moving scenes the mask aligns perfectly. For rapid panning the sky mask trails by 1–2 frames. This is a conscious trade-off — running the network on every frame would drain the battery and heat the device much faster.

I tapped the shutter but no recording saved.

A few possibilities:

  • Photo library permission missing. iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → SkyFi → Add Photos Only. SkyFi only needs add-only; it never reads your library.
  • Out of storage. Recording requires at least ~10 MB free for a 10-second clip.
  • Tapped too quickly. The first 1–2 frames after tapping the shutter may not capture while the encoder initializes. Hold for at least one second before tapping again to stop.

If you see a green REC chip at the top of the screen with a timer counting up, recording is live. If you see the white circle shutter button (not red), recording is not currently active.

The front camera doesn't flip when I tap the flip button.

Camera flip only works on physical iPhones. The iOS Simulator does not have front/back cameras, so the button is a no-op there.

Why is the cityscape silhouette occluding the UFO?

That's the feature working as designed. SkyFi's segmentation identifies what is sky vs. foreground; lens effects only render in the sky region. So if a UFO drifts behind a building, the building will visibly hide its lower half — the same way a real object in the sky would be occluded.

Permissions reference

Camera

Required for the live preview. Granted on first launch. Revoke via iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera → SkyFi. Without it SkyFi shows the "SKYFI CALIBRATION ERROR / AUTHORIZE NEURAL SENSORS" screen.

Photos — Add Only

Required for saving recorded videos. Granted the first time you record. Revoke via iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → SkyFi. SkyFi requests add-only access — it cannot read or view your existing library.

Device requirements

  • iPhone with iOS 16.0 or later.
  • Apple Neural Engine recommended (iPhone XS / 2018 or newer) for best segmentation speed.
  • Roughly 50 MB free disk space for the app and bundled neural network model.

Privacy & data

SkyFi does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal data. Every camera frame is processed on-device. See the Privacy Policy for full details.

Still stuck?

Email support@skyfi.example with:

  • iPhone model (Settings → General → About → Model Name).
  • iOS version (Settings → General → About → iOS Version).
  • SkyFi version (visible at the top of the app: NEURAL CAMERA PROTOCOL v1.0.x).
  • A screenshot or short screen-recording of the issue.
  • What you were trying to do and what happened instead.

We typically respond within 2 business days. SkyFi is a small project and there's no auto-responder bot in the loop.
